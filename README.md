# One Letter for Your Day

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-one-letter-for-your-day-e54e9c36): exactly one letter, A to Z, standing for the word that describes your day.

**The letter: C — for "chaotic".**

Everything happening at once, nothing going to plan, and you lived to tell it. Chaotic paints a sharper picture than a vague mood, lands as a joke you repeat to a friend, and is true of any real day that went sideways — and C is its natural initial.

`main.py` writes the single letter to `/output/entry.txt`, self-checks the gate (exactly one ASCII letter A–Z, no newline, no whitespace), and re-reads the file to verify the bytes.
